## Delegating complex research

Any agent MAY search and inspect the codebase and consult specific online sources as part of its work.

You MUST delegate research in either of these cases.

- Collecting sources or analyzing collected evidence is a separate research deliverable.
- The work requires an open-ended survey that involves discovering and selecting a body of source material. Include any research analysis requested for the survey in the delegation.

When delegating source collection, you MUST assign it to the agent responsible for finding and extracting source material.

When delegating research analysis, you MUST assign it to the agent responsible for analyzing supplied material.
